Busbar Fundamentals

Busbars are solid metallic conductors used to collect and distribute large amounts of electrical power within a panel. They are typically made of copper or aluminum due to their excellent conductivity. The sizing of a busbar depends not only on the continuous current but also on the short-circuit withstand strength and the maximum allowable temperature rise.

Design Standards

  • IPS-E-EL-110: Iranian Petroleum Standard for industrial electrical installations and panel layouts.
  • Publication 110: National standard for building electrical installations and wiring regulations.
  • IEC 61439: General requirements for low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies.

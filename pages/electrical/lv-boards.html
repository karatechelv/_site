LV Distribution Boards

Architecture, components, and standards for Low Voltage main and sub-distribution panels.

Types of LV Boards

LV boards are categorized based on their location in the distribution hierarchy. Main Distribution Boards (MDB) receive power directly from the transformer and distribute it to Sub-Distribution Boards (SDB). Motor Control Centers (MCC) are specialized panels dedicated to controlling and protecting multiple electric motors.

Key Panel Components

  • Busbars: Copper or aluminum conductors for distributing high currents within the panel.
  • Circuit Breakers: MCCB and MCB devices for protecting circuits against overloads and short circuits.
  • Contactors & Relays: Electromechanical switches for controlling motors and lighting circuits.

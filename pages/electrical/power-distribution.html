Power Distribution & Transformers

Architecture of MV/LV networks and interactive calculators for industrial power delivery.

Distribution Networks (MV/LV)

Industrial power distribution involves stepping down high voltage from the grid to medium voltage (MV), typically 11kV or 33kV, and then to low voltage (LV) 400V for end-use equipment. Proper architecture ensures reliability, minimizes power loss, and allows for maintenance isolation without shutting down the entire facility.

Transformer Fundamentals

Transformers operate on the principle of electromagnetic induction to transfer AC power between circuits at different voltage levels without changing the frequency. In industrial panels, dry-type and oil-immersed transformers are commonly used to step down MV to LV. Correct sizing, based on total connected load and future expansion, is critical to prevent overheating and ensure operational longevity.

Key Substation Components

  • MV Switchgear: Metal-enclosed panels housing circuit breakers, disconnectors, and protection relays.
  • Power Transformers: Step-down units connecting the MV network to the LV main switchgear.
  • LV Main Distribution Boards: Assemblies containing main breakers, busbars, and outgoing feeders.

Overview of Electrical Engineering

Comprehensive insights into power systems, industrial automation, protection coordination, and ELV infrastructure.

Core Discipline in Modern Industry

Electrical engineering in industrial and ELV (Extra-Low Voltage) contexts is the backbone of modern infrastructure. It encompasses the design, analysis, and maintenance of power distribution networks, control systems, and safety circuits. It ensures the reliable delivery of power, precise control of machinery, and integral safety of personnel and assets.

Specialized Branches

  • Power Distribution & Transformers: Design of MV/LV networks, substation architecture, and transformer sizing.
  • Protection & Coordination: Selective coordination of breakers, fuses, and relays to isolate faults instantly.
  • ELV Systems: Fire alarm, CCTV, access control, and public address systems for building safety.
  • Power Quality & Harmonics: Mitigation of THD, power factor correction, and voltage stability analysis.
